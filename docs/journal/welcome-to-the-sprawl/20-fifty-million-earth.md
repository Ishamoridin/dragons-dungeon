# Session 20 — Fifty million Earth

## [S20.1] The Director's strike and the four gates
- Campaign time: 3500-10-09T10:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz
- Location: Verge, the Processing Plant

The Director's bird was simple: "Fifty million Earth, density of Reishi comparable to the Air palace, seventy percent of Essence Tower density. Guaranteed capability to compromise and destroy hostile mega-structure. Duration of assault required: fifteen seconds." Glitz: "Any damage to the One True Path predicted?" "Unknown essences in play. Sixty percent probability of survivable damage, five percent possibility of critical damage. Acceptable odds." "What's the remaining thirty-five percent?" "No damage. Pairing with administrative user also lowers probability."

Gym rolled through release, brawl, metallurgy, crafting, intelligence, occult, resolve, zeal, kharma and luck, and then a single enormous die that the Director halved. The Wall shuddered; its stability dipped below zero and the Breach reacted. Frollen the Rabbit's release carried the ritual. Conqueror Krag, Sovereign of Earth, rose for a moment to be killed, lost, augmented and inverted all at once.

Sylas asked how bad it would be if the Heart-Kin reached the third and fourth gate. "Impossible calculation, insufficient historical information." "Not even you know what they do?" "First gate: Reincarnation. Second gate: The Queue. Third gate: The Sire. Fourth gate: Unknown." Pluck the Rooster muttered something about art being wasted on plebeians. Sylas asked whether any Heart-Kin were coming now the gate was revealed. "Two unknown figures, impossible Reiryoku density, detected at Verge gates. Verbem detected unconscious, teleport successful. Five minutes until intercept." Gym whispered to Glitz, "A thousand tier one says this goes wrong." Glitz: "Oooh, bad odds. I like it."

**Outcomes**
- The Processing Plant of Verge compromised by the Director's 50-million-Earth strike.
- Lore: the four gates (Reincarnation, The Queue, The Sire, Unknown).
- Krag: killed/inverted.

## [S20.2] Kaeldur, Zerynthia, Glimmer
- Campaign time: 3500-10-09T11:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz
- Location: Verge gates

Sylas asked the Wall why he had been given two paths to reincarnate. "You have denied yourself reincarnation until your role as a Zodiac is complete, past, present and future. The Tenth must be the last." He chose: to live the life he did, reincarnate as himself, the member of the Tenth Wave he was. Evil's only insight: stopping something costs Kaeldur proportionately to attempting it. And then: "You define yourself. Guidance hasn't mattered before. A path has been offered. Choice is personal. A demand was obeyed. Zodiac self-define, the gate will end, the end will begin, the war follows. Zodiac will win, or the Heart will end this Wall."

Kaeldur struck Will directly: twelve hundred and fifty, then three thousand five hundred and ninety, halved by Glitz's work. Gym's brawl broke fifty. Zerynthia's release at thirty-four picked between Gym and an ally and chose Gym; her Glimmer's Hunt and a twenty for "personal touch" summoned Glimmer, who dealt twenty-four thousand of true Chaos damage across the field and another two thousand true on the follow-up, then distributed twenty-one thousand more with armour applying. Gym took nine thousand of it and kept standing on adaptation alone. Glimmer rewrote Glitz's mind for seven turns into believing the other Zodiac were Heart-Kin. Flock and the Director poured sixty-seven thousand of healing and force into the gap.

Glitz spent his Mote of Impossibility: "to be the Glitz right here, right now, free of injury." Fully healed.

**Outcomes**
- Sylas: reincarnation path chosen (himself, Tenth Wave).
- Glitz: Mote of Impossibility spent; fully healed. Suffered 7 turns of Glimmer's delusion.
- Gym: absorbed 9,260 of Glimmer's Chaos; Rei pool briefly over 1,100 per repair.

### Table notes
- Several brb/bio breaks from Gym and Glitz mid-fight.

## [S20.3] Inevitability and Breach
- Campaign time: 3500-10-09T15:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz
- Location: Frollen's reactor, north of Verge

What Glimmer left behind was a recipe. Essence of Violation plus five unknowns plus Essence of Breach equals Essence of Inevitability, unstable: nine thousand of it. Fifty thousand Steel. Four hundred and twenty-one thousand Surprise. Frollen's reactor to the north read: an auto-harvester for Magic; a reactor fed Violation, Surprise, Insight, an unknown, Breach and Steel to produce Inevitability; a second auto-harvester for Improbable. Harvest: five hundred Violation, ninety-one thousand Insight, three thousand Magic, nine hundred Improbable, and Breach by separate rolls of resolve, constitution and control.

Each of them learned the signature of an impossible essence and, as Zodiac, folded it into themselves: one Breach each, the ability to convert any tier five to Breach one for one, and the capacity to wield one without immediate disaster. The Director read the room: Heart 17/100, Chaos 30/100, Finality 90/100, Fixation 5/100.

The Wall paid for Earth's military, the Rifted, Krag, the Heart-Kin and Glimmer: seven hundred and thirty-six development to seventeen slots of their choice each, ten universal points each, Kido/Hado uses, and personal adjustments: Gym's True Resero maintenance set to zero, Glitz's First Resero to zero, Sylas a hundred development uses to a field of choice.

**Outcomes**
- Essences: 9,000 Inevitability (unstable), 50,000 Steel, 421,584 Surprise, 500 Violation, 91,512 Insight, 3,405 Magic, 917 Improbable; 1 Breach each.
- Trait: Essence of Breach (convert tier 5 to Breach 1:1; wield 1 safely).
- Rewards: +736 to 17 chosen development slots each; +10 USP each; 29 Kido/Hado uses; Gym True Resero maintain 0; Glitz First Resero maintain 0; Sylas +100 development uses; Glitz +50 to all bar maxima.
