# Session 06 — The hungering source

## [S06.1] Under the river
- Campaign time: 3500-07-06T11:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Water source, beneath the township

The party went down to the source of the township's water and found it sick. Sylas went under first and stayed under too long; Crash bellowed "SYLAS YER DROWNIN'! SAVE YOURSELF!" while Sylas, calm as ever, swam on. The pull on all of them was a thing the DM named the Hungering Vacuum, and resisting it took Reiryoku control, empathy, occult knowledge and wisdom at once. Glitz held his control perfectly; his occult sense failed him. Vinnie held on with his wits more than his power.

At the waterfall Glitz inspected the essence itself. Water was still vastly superior here, but it was crashing against something else as it arrived and both were being reduced. The other essence came from the vines. Glitz floated out over the underground river to see if the clash continued downstream.

Vinnie tried to help and hurt himself instead, as he kept doing. Crash wondered aloud whether that was a theme or a secret of the spirit weapon, since it was how Vinnie had died.

**Outcomes**
- Finding: Water at the source is clashing with Life essence from the vines; both are being consumed.
- Vinnie: self-inflicted damage during the dive.

### Table notes
- Crash's player: "it's wild how often your actions hurt yourself." Glitz found his own failed empathy roll funny.
- Crash (Hollow) lit a cigarette in a scene; Crash: "Aw damn, he's got smokes. BYOS."

## [S06.2] The silhouette with a tail
- Campaign time: 3500-07-06T12:30
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Water source

A silhouette formed in the water ahead of Crash. It had no detail yet, but it was shaping bubbles of air around him so he could breathe, and there was clearly room in its outline for long pointed ears and a tail. Vinnie, who had been cultivating Fire, stopped when the figure stepped ashore and became Kaeris of the Fallen Pack.

Her release hit Vinnie for three hundred and seventy-four, and he gurgled gently. She sat down at the water's edge, posture tight and ready, eyes following anyone who moved. Crash asked if it was really that bad; they still had the river. Glitz had worked it out: "You created a Life zone over the Nature zone, right? I thought that was just to constrain the world. But no, the vines reaching toward the Pilgrims' Gate! The initial plan was to infect this zone with Life."

Crash borrowed Glitz's flight and whooped across the cavern ("HOOOO BABY WE'RE MAKIN' IT HAPPEN"). Vinnie flitted about converting every scrap of loose reishi into smoke and gathering it in one place, quietly trying to craft something that could strike a heart.

**Outcomes**
- Kaeris revealed as the shape beneath the source. Vinnie took 374 from her release.
- Glitz's deduction: the Life zone was seeded to infect Nature via the vines toward the Pilgrims' Gate.

### Table notes
- Glitz: "ah, the Persona 2 twist." Sylas stepped away.

## [S06.3] The litany and the Crux of Fire
- Campaign time: 3500-07-06T14:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Water source

As he worked the smoke, Vinnie muttered a litany ingrained in him as a Hollow, soothing at first and gathering certainty: "Fire burns, earth endures, hope perseveres, courage defies, water flows, nature grows, air escapes, insight learns, beast acts, surprise violates, doom determines, gloom obscures, evil controls, order defines, determination ignores, drive motivates, Steel crafts, Resolve emboldens, Vitality invigorates, Light illuminates, Night obfuscates, Despair defeats, Corruption undoes, Ambition expands, Miracle wishes, Magic wants, Improbable occurs, Inevitable looms, Creation creates, Entropy decays, Harmony balances, but above all, Hearts break."

His zeal and presence both failed him at the end, and the DM sent him a private truth: the Crux of Fire. The flame still burns. It has not yet become the fire of today and must be reignited. Vinnie will do so, as he chooses, and become a Fire Hollow, or the Heart will remember being broken. One day it could be a hex or a pact.

Kaeris asked Vinnie to shape for her at a cost of two hundred and fifty Reiryoku, and he did it superbly, reishi manipulation and weaponry both sharp even where his empathy fell short. Something else, unnamed, watched the exchange with an empathy and a cheating presence far above any of theirs. Lady Soar read the room with care. Sylas and Glitz were tested on piety and presence and found wanting; Glitz got a strange feeling he had been insulted. The DM set a rule for Fire in the body: every mote costs a point of health but grants two Will and one to the pool.

**Outcomes**
- Vinnie: "The Crux of Fire" (private note; convertible to a Hex/Pact when the method is found).
- Vinnie: shaped for Kaeris (250 Reiryoku).
- Rule: Fire physical application: 1 Fire costs 1 HP, grants 2 Will and 1 pool.
