# Session 04 — The Township of Water

## [S04.1] Tea, a clerk, and a mutation
- Campaign time: 3500-07-04T10:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Glitz's home and the Water administrative hub

The Township of Water took them in the way it takes in anyone Glitz vouches for. Aureon, Glitz's husband, returned from somewhere with a tray of green Nature tea, set it between Vinnie and Glitz without a word, and began dusting the room. A cup restored a little of everything.

At the administrative hub, Clerk Phiré examined Vinnie's chaotic Reiryoku and told him flatly that it would do one of three things: mutate, manifest, or explode. It mutated. From that morning on Vinnie's reishi was chaotic in a useful way: any tier-one essence he held could be converted into any other, for a trickle of Reiryoku per mote. Phiré stamped a receipt for five hundred Surprise, dated the fourth of July, and Vinnie smoked his last drag, flicked the butt at the wall, stamped on the smoulder and wandered off as his attention drifted.

Grocer Rasma sold them five weeks of rations, grains and vegetables and fish protein enough for a high-class dish a day.

**Outcomes**
- Vinnie: Mutative trait "Chaotic Reishi" (convert any tier 1 essence into any other at 5 Reiryoku per mote).
- Receipt: 500 Surprise, 04/07/35XX.
- Item: 5x weekly rations (size 3 each).

### Table notes
- Aureon's tea rule: a cup restores 40 to all three bars.

## [S04.2] Flock refuses the gate
- Campaign time: 3500-07-04T13:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Township of Water, outside Flock's lodge

A scavenger with too much Reiatsu squared up to Vinnie in the street and found Vinnie's presence and resolve harder than expected. Glitz talked everyone down.

Then there was Flock. The Liar Bird, Sovereign of Evil, gave Crash a stalwart, stubborn stare and said: "I have NO intention of using ANY gate. I am a font, an outsider, a SOVEREIGN. I will be staying even when, if, you go." Louri, a priest of Water, tried piety on him and got nothing. Glitz, reading from academics, recalled the Liar's Cloak: an Evil-Kin can wrap their aura in a field of disorder that subverts intended outcomes, for a silent Evil cost. Flock was wearing one.

### Table notes
- DM stepped out briefly; players were told there were no ambushes and could move freely.
- Vinnie's player went brb/back during the scavenger standoff.

## [S04.3] The Heart's pulse and a virus for the Strangle
- Campaign time: 3500-07-04T17:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Township of Water

Something with a strong Reiatsu release brushed the township, and in its wake Kaeris, a Beast-Kin the party had only heard of, laid out two techniques as if teaching a class. Heart Pulse: a hundred and fifty Will and fifteen hundred Heart essence to restore five hundred health and Reiryoku in one action. Canker Pulse: a hundred Will and five hundred Surprise to deal five hundred true damage to any Heart-Kin using the first. Glitz took the lesson in with sharp manipulation and sharper arithmetic.

By agreement with the Zodiac, Flock released an Evil virus into the Strangle to consume its Life essence. Evil could now feed on the Nature of the former Sacred Forest and no longer had to cultivate or drain the Water rivers. Vinnie, satisfied, walked up to Crash with the backpack he had been lent. "Thanks for lending me this, bud. Want some new shoes?"

**Outcomes**
- Techniques observed: Heart Pulse (cast on Reiatsu release 19+) and Canker Pulse.
- World: Flock's Evil virus consumes the Strangle's Life; Evil shifts to Nature from the Sacred Forest.
- Glitz: 4,600 computed from a 23,000 Surprise payout divided five ways.

### Table notes
- Glitz worked out the Surprise split in chat (23,000 / 5).
- Session wound down on "ready" calls, then the DM started an ambience stream for anyone who wanted it.
