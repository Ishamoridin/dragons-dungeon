# Session 18 — Birds on a Saturday

## [S18.1] Ashamori's gifts and the Tiger's sparring
- Campaign time: 3500-10-05T10:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Wandering Tavern

Ashamori distributed gifts with a Midnight Orchid's glow behind each: for Gym, the Entropic Knuckler brawling weapon and a Roundabout Token that lets a member of a Wave respawn at will for ten universal points, as a later wave; for Lick, a Zero-Spark that heals fully from death once; for Glitz, a Mote of Impossibility that can do anything up to five hundred of any Rei effect could, as a minor action, free of essence; for Flock, a Blank Cheque good for one costless action on behalf of the Realm.

Flit, Tiger of Courage, and Lick, Dragon of Fire, sparred with Sylas in the yard. Flit rolled perfect threes in a row and then hit for nearly fifteen thousand with a physical that stacked fourteen times; Sylas's counters at thirty-two and thirty-six kept him in it, and he gave back nine hundred with the dance. Lick's brawl at forty-four and his five-iteration strike for sixteen hundred ended the lesson.

Glitz lay on a couch with the headset unplugged, asked for cliff notes, and left when his boyfriend arrived.

**Outcomes**
- Gym: Entropic Knuckler; Roundabout Token. Glitz: Mote of Impossibility. Lick: Zero-Spark. Flock: Blank Cheque.
- Gym: 1,805 HP, 276 Will, 439 Rei recovered; 7,131 essence from Ashamori.

## [S18.2] Heart to attack Heart
- Campaign time: 3500-10-05T14:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Wandering Tavern

Flock, with two allies and Evil overcharged, drew four thousand of Rei and Will out of the air. Verbem, called "the Rat" now, matched him on wisdom. Zerynthia, a Heart-Kin, watched with a zeal that did not waver. Sylas relayed what Flock had said: he was using Heart to attack Heart inside his towers. That would cause a heart attack, and every part of the world corrupted by Heart would be destroyed with it. He knew. Air would be safe, and Evil. He did not care about Water or Night.

Lady Soar's Water bird arrived: "On behalf of the Township of Water, I hereby rescind the Abyssal authority of Sylas the Goat. Water will recognise the Zodiac as neutral, but identifies Sylas as acting beyond his jurisdiction and endangering the stability of the Southern Sprawl. Water declares voluntary vassalisation under the Nation of Evil to gain immunity to the Heart-Failure."

Cross's Night bird followed: the Night-Kin had voted; the Southern Sprawl, Air and now the Fire alliance was the best path; Flock was immune to diplomatic offence unless he directly harmed a nation that had declared against the Heart-Kin; in Zodiac-versus-Sovereign matters the greater Kin would not choose sides, and would choose Flock if pressed. "Stand down."

Gym began to stir.

**Outcomes**
- Water rescinds Sylas's Abyssal authority and vassalises itself to Evil.
- Night declares neutrality between Zodiac and Sovereigns, favouring Flock if forced.

## [S18.3] The Stint and the Director's projection
- Campaign time: 3500-10-05T16:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Wandering Tavern

Flock explained the Stint. The Canker had been spreading pulses and signals through buried hearts across the whole Wall. Flock had secretly sent out Standard Bearers which, when the Sacred Monster consumed them, taught it the scent of the Heart-Kin roots. Air, Water, Night, Evil, Nature and forty percent of Earth were now free of the Heart-Kin's corruptive pulses. The Evil Essence Towers sat on the major artery junctions as bottlenecks, limiting their own offence to stall the Heart-Kin at huge cost.

The Director zoned out, then spoke in a slow analytical drone. Heart contamination across the Wall: seven hundred motes a day, down from a historic peak of seventy-five thousand five months ago. The remainder was concentrated in Fire and in the Processing Plant of Verge in Earth and the Impossible Flame Crater of Fire. Containing Fire would cut it to two hundred. Redirected motes must be returned to sender; the resulting heart attack would deal five times the resisted motes per day to whoever pulsed them. Projection: four weeks of heart attack before the Canker's outer structure failed. Urgent: within two weeks of that, an eighty-five percent chance the Heart-Kin would act to eliminate external threats.

Flock's cultivation ceremony and war-chest rolls left the Evil public coffers at five million one hundred and seventy thousand. Carrion, elsewhere, fought the Frenzied with Doom cascading in every direction.

**Outcomes**
- Evil public coffers: 5,170,000.
- Projection: Canker outer structure fails in ~4 weeks; Heart-Kin offensive likely within 2 weeks after.

## [S18.4] Saturday's birds
- Campaign time: 3500-10-06T09:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz
- Location: The Wandering Tavern

Carrion sent a Surprise bird to each of the three core Zodiac: "Battle with Frenzied yesterday went... um, 'explosively'. Frenzied Plains are now the Doomlands. Official military alliance signed with Aureon's New Flame Empire." Gym replied with a bird containing a thumbs up. Emperor Aureon's bird came Saturday morning, calm: New Flame confirmed the defeat of the Ferno Frenzied; Carrion the Doombringer had requested and received the Empire's support to build his own society in the Doomlands. Clanken's bird came Saturday afternoon, cold and concise: "Zodiac team 'Reserata' positioned south of Verge. Awaiting team Plenum."

Sylas answered Clanken that they were on their way and could reach it with the Tower, and asked Carrion how he was. "...A lot of souls got reincarnated. I've got about seventy Doomed around me now. Feeling a bit dejected." "Is there anything you need?" "Time." "Understood. Reach out if anything changes." The Director added that Vexorion had triggered a recall spell during his battle with Lazarus and could no longer be detected, and Lazarus had retreated to the Tavern to heal. Then: unstable essences detected. Mutation, Reversion, Subversion, Camouflage, Abomination, Rupture, Rift, Inversion, Consumption, and more than fifteen others that denied categorisation.

**Outcomes**
- World: the Frenzied Plains become the Doomlands; Doom allied with New Flame.
- Development awarded: 30 Constitution, 25 Reiatsu and Rei control, 20 Zeal, Faith and Empathy.
- Gym: 362 healed through Glitz's tally; armour repaired.
