# Session 03 — The scav camp

## [S03.1] Three natural twenties
- Campaign time: 3500-07-02T21:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Scavenger camp, a dense warren of tents and shacks

The scavs saw them coming, but not well enough. Sylas opened the fight flawlessly, Vinnie slipped a scout's strike as if he had rehearsed it, and Glitz's grip on his Reiryoku was perfect: three natural twenties in a row before a single enemy had acted. Crash summoned his deck and drew a hand of sevens, fives and eights.

The scavs recovered fast. A scout turned Vinnie's own Reiatsu back on him and then landed a blow that would have killed anyone without armour; Vinnie's jacket, somehow, held. Glitz noted aloud that he could not have survived that. Sylas carved two scav gunners down with heavy, methodical strikes. Crash healed with a card, then granted free movement with another.

Vinnie, advancing on the scout, laid one hand on the shack wall. It contracted, liquefied and melted. A quarter of the building was gone in moments and five charges of smoke swirled around his fist. The scout dodged his first swing and ate the second.

**Outcomes**
- Vinnie: five smoke charges from the melted shack; took 197 from the scout's counter (armour held).
- Sylas: two scav gunners badly wounded (116 and 115 damage).
- Crash: 42 healed to an ally via card.

### Table notes
- Glitz stepped away and asked for a quick recap on return. Sylas asked if three player nat 20s in a row earned anything special.
- Vinnie's player credited "dragon armour" for surviving the 197.

## [S03.2] The Liar Bird's clone and the helmet in the dirt
- Campaign time: 3500-07-02T23:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Scavenger camp

Mid-fight, Flock arrived, or something wearing his shape: a reishi clone that brawled a skirmisher into the ground and shrugged off its own fate costs with a Sovereign's stubbornness. Sylas recognised it for what it was. From the background Glitz waved and announced "Hi, I'm Glitz!", which did nothing for anyone's composure. The camp fell.

The map of the warren was far more accurate than it had any right to be, and the four picked through it. The loot was a thousand each of Water, Nature, Beast and Insight and five hundred of Fire. In the dirt Vinnie found an intact, freshly forged half-helm: a Rush Guard pattern, favoured by the Sheer's Guard of Water's military a hundred years ago, which should not have been fresh at all.

**Outcomes**
- Party: 1,000 Water, 1,000 Nature, 1,000 Beast, 1,000 Insight, 500 Fire.
- Item: Rush Guard Helmet (intact, newly forged, century-old pattern).

### Table notes
- Glitz briefly introduced himself as "Glam" before correcting to "Glitz".
- Glitz's player reminded the table that only tier-one techniques are known from the start; others must be trained, though development points still accrue.
