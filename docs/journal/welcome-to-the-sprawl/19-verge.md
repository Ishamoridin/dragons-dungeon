# Session 19 — Verge

## [S19.1] Earth's military
- Campaign time: 3500-10-08T10:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz
- Location: South of Verge, Earth

Team Reserata met them: Flit, Lick, Malla the Dog of Beast and Clanken the Monkey of Insight. Earth's military was waiting. Snipers whose rounds petrified anyone who failed a resolve or zeal test, shutting their spirit weapon off entirely. A templar whose Barren Strike shaved armour and maximum bars by a quarter of the damage dealt. Bulwarks pouring out Reiatsu damage in the high hundreds. Weavers who sacrificed their own to call down Earth's Command for over a thousand.

Gym brawled at thirty-five and his strikes passed two hundred, two Earth weavers down. Lick's Rising Fire-Strike landed for fourteen hundred and his leap carried him across the field. Flit's Stinger's Special hit twice for nine hundred and eight hundred. Malla's weapon dealt six hundred and seventy-six per swing and his Baying for Blood reached thirty-eight. Clanken's ranged attacks at forty, twice, chose Entropy. Glitz's control held at thirty-plus while he shepherded everyone's survival. Sylas kept the counters coming.

Gym repaired his armour between every exchange until the DM asked whether a times-eight multiplier was meant to apply to injuries.

**Outcomes**
- Earth military routed at Verge. Technique observed: Petrification (Resolve or Zeal 23+ to avoid; deactivates spirit weapons).
- Malla: Reaction essence (inputs Fulfilment, Victory, Mutation, Evolution; 10,000 per day).
- Rolls for new essence: Sylas 1,471 / 1,915; Glitz 791 / 1,214; Gym 1,104 / 604.
