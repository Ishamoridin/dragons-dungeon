# Session 14 — Shimmering Sands

## [S14.1] Hotwire
- Campaign time: 3500-09-08T10:00
- Present: Hotwire, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The road to Shimmering Sands

Seven weeks later the man who had been Vinnie called himself Hotwire: still a Hollow, now wearing the shape of an InduDrake, a Spirit Beast of smoke and iron that could draw on nothing but Steel and Industry. He turned to Glitz as Crash walked off: "Ay, you know I was just kiddin' about not givin' you a boon. You want it?" The Truthseer noted that Crash's soul had adapted to a flood of Wraith-drain and would now take only a quarter from it. Ashmaw tested the party's release and found Crash sharpest.

Hotwire's first attempt to make a companion failed; the second produced Screw, a tiny white helix-moving copy of himself, given the instinct to find useful objects and the ability to convert inert reishi into smoke and feed it back to Hotwire within range. As Sylas drew out Nature essence a weight settled on the world like a gravity well, and when he consumed it a ripple of restoration passed through everyone before the weight vanished.

**Outcomes**
- Crash: Wraith Resistance (25% damage to Rei and Will from Wraith-drain).
- Hotwire: construct "Screw".
- Metals found: Scouring Steel, an unknown steel and an unknown green alloy.

### Table notes
- Hotwire's player: "He coulda been a Wayfinder, dontchaknow." Crash asked if Ashmaw's effect was presence-based (no).

## [S14.2] The Theory of Shimmering Sands
- Campaign time: 3500-09-08T12:00
- Present: Hotwire, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Shimmering Sands, outer works

Sylas found the book. Shimmering Sands was founded by Air with Fire's help, to test whether, if both ignored the primacy of their own essence, they could build better defences against the Essence Tower and mega-structures like it than either could alone. When the old Tower fell the Spire Guard joined, and their Harmony crafting opened an era of realm-distorting but not corrupting weaponry. Shimmering Sands is itself a mega-structure built around a surviving Essence Tower, using it as an engine for a self-sufficient town and a factory producing Human-Killer grade weapons and above. The book ended: "It worked too well. We're doomed."

Crash caught Hotwire at a bridge: "That bridge will kill you, man, don't cross it. Not without prep." Hotwire: "Seems like it, yeah. Who we fightin' anyway?"

Then five perfectly two-dimensional lines of cobalt light strobed into being above the tower, revolved, converged and burst white. From the flash stepped a lycan humanoid rubbing his chin, robed in flickering blue data, his eyes literally flashing with calculation as he inspected each Zodiac and Sovereign present. The tower echoed him, forming and dissolving blue symbols every second. The tower was awake. The Director stepped down from Crash's palm, grew to full size and regarded Crash with open intrigue.

**Outcomes**
- Lore: The Theory of Shimmering Sands (as above).
- The Director awakens; the Essence Tower is active.

### Table notes

## [S14.3] Reimalgam
- Campaign time: 3500-09-08T14:00
- Present: Hotwire, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The cavern before the factory

The reishi of the cavern bulged and pulsed, pooling at the top of each ramp into Reimalgam humanoids: warriors who fired in volleys and wanderers who hit for hundreds and once for eight hundred and seven. Glitz took that one and lived with a hundred of it shaved off. Hotwire brawled through them and shaped smoke for three hundred at a time. Crash's deck, now dealing eights with regularity, healed three hundred and seventy-four in one card. Sylas drew on fifty thousand Nature.

Crash to the dragon at his side: "Hey my son, will you follow me around and fight the people I fight? It's kinda cool to have a knight fight with a dragon by his side, but chill until we're fightin' folks?" It would.

**Outcomes**
- Reimalgam cleared. Crash: 374 healed via card.

## [S14.4] Thorne's vault
- Campaign time: 3500-09-08T17:00
- Present: Hotwire, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The factory barracks

The Spire-Guard Templar did not use a keycard. He inserted the tip of his blade into the door and turned, and as it opened a wave of pure passion, hope and delight, of anything being possible, rolled out and he screamed as that much improbability eroded his Construct nature. Inside was a barracks: bunks, desks, dining tables, work benches, one lit CRT, and a man in the most ornate full suit of armour ever designed. Thorne.

He hit like nothing they had faced: brawl at thirty-seven and thirty-eight. Crash met him at forty-four and stayed standing; Hotwire's brawl reached forty-three. Crash's strikes climbed past eight hundred. Thorne's zeal cracked and Hotwire's with it. The vault opened: a Human-Killer "Phoenix Fire" Mote of Power; a Human-Killer construct seed called Reitan; two Harmony-grade riot suits, "Wave 10's Justice"; and an Essence Tower Seed.

Thorne spent a hundred Heart to teleport the moment the vault opened, but first asked, "What do I do to the Heart-Kin when I return?" Glitz offered: "Be a mole. Sabotage from inside for us. Be subtle, but be ready to help." One artery of the Heart had turned on the whole, and the faction now called Thorne's Artery was in open conflict with the other Heart-Kin. The world resented having to count one group of Heart-Kin as part of the solution, but deferred to the Zodiac.

The factory fell idle when he left. Its terminal can command it to produce Harmony-grade war-form equipment, utilities, food, even the components of a mega-structure; it works because The One True Path defers potential to it, and whoever holds the administrative console inherits that potential.

Vinnie, or Hotwire, did not walk out. The DM's notes spoke of "surviving characters" and of new characters scaled to the party average. The Zodiac left with the Tower Seed and one fewer of their own.

**Outcomes**
- Vault: Phoenix Fire Mote of Power (1 burden); Reitan seed (1 burden, Rei Manip 20+ to spawn); 2x Wave 10's Justice riot suits (5 pieces, 4 burden each); Essence Tower Seed (1 burden).
- World: Thorne's Artery turns against the Heart-Kin. The factory's administrative console commands its output.
- Rewards for survivors: 14,160 tier 1; 214,703 tier 2; 483 up to tier 3; 100,000 Evil.
- Vinnie/Hotwire: dead.
