# Session 05 — "COP! DERES A COP!"

## [S05.1] Something tried to be sensed
- Campaign time: 3500-07-05T10:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Township of Water, outside Flock's lodge

Glitz leaned against a wall pretending to smoke. Flock stood at the door of his lodge, hand inches from the handle, arguing silently behind soundproof walls with a very worried scavenger. And all four Zodiac, without deciding to, reached out with their feelings at the same moment. Crash and Vinnie caught the edge of it. Sylas caught nothing. None of them knew they had done it until afterwards. Verbem watched, raised a hand to speak, and then smiled and waited to see if they would work it out themselves. He chuckled.

### Table notes
- DM: "No one is consciously aware of the fact until afterwards." Sylas stepped away for a drink.

## [S05.2] Vinnie dies, and does not stop
- Campaign time: 3500-07-05T11:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Township of Water

Vinnie tallied up everything he had scraped together, more than ten thousand motes of mixed essence, and did something with it that nobody at the table ever fully explained. The luck went cold. Glitz, deadpan, ticked another mark on his list titled "amount of times Vinnie has tried to kill himself". This time it worked.

What stood up afterwards was still Vinnie. Composed, cigarette lit, his old body replaced by the hollow shape of a thing that remembered being a man and was not sorry. From that hour he called himself Vinnie the Hollow, and his smoke obeyed him better than it ever had.

Crash Vandal looked at the Hollow, looked at Flock, and panicked. "COP! DERES A COP!" Verbem sighed and facepalmed. Flock whispered, "...WHAT have YOU done...?" Sylas quietly told Flock to get behind him. Vinnie shrugged and leaned against the wall, smoking slowly. Crash checked his gun, discovered it wasn't loaded, bent over with his hands on his knees and announced he was going to puke.

**Outcomes**
- Vinnie dies and returns as Vinnie (Hollow). Total essence counted at death: 10,099.

### Table notes
- Sylas: "time for the friendship beam". The DM loved that Flock got a "fucking amateurs" moment.
- Vinnie's player linked the shared rules workbook in chat.

## [S05.3] Glitz's house
- Campaign time: 3500-07-05T13:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Glitz's home

Glitz led them home and nearly crossed his arms in frustration before remembering that would calm the whole street. Crash tapped the floor for trapdoors. "Not a bad place you have here," Sylas offered. "Pretty homely." Glitz: "It was bigger in the past. Made sacrifices as things got worse."

Crash wanted secret passages. Glitz didn't have any; that was the style of Fire or Water, not Surprise, and besides he had a good relationship with the people of Water. "Oh, so you paid OFF the cops." Sylas asked about security and Glitz explained that living with a Fire-Kin and two children mostly raised by said Fire-Kin kept the place secure enough; half the time he walked in to a loving right hook. The kids were physically sixteen and eighteen and had been around for maybe a hundred years. They were made before resources got scarce. He couldn't remember exactly when.

A friendly spar broke out. Glitz landed two solid hits and dodged well. Crash tried a sucker punch and fumbled it completely. Aureon's attempt to intimidate the room failed so gently it was almost affectionate. Glitz thanked him for his advice: "You are reliable as always, my love."

### Table notes
- Sylas's caps lock got stuck mid-line. Sylas took a bio break.
- DM apologised in advance for Aureon's accent, meant to mark him as from Fire, not to offend.
- Sylas recognised the "Mercenary" NPC as a nod to a friend's avatar; Vinnie's player replied "Cousin".

## [S05.4] Water's token and a talk about fear
- Campaign time: 3500-07-05T18:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Township of Water, Lady Soar's tent

Lady Soar took out a blank piece of reishi fabric, imprinted it with waves rushing upward, and offered it to the group. Vinnie touched his temple in mock salute and muttered to Glitz, "Ain't he called General?" Commander Dahl set aside five thousand for Sylas. A Relief Corps healer saw to the worst of the morning.

Crash found Vinnie. "Hey man, sorry for freakin' out earlier. Like I said, I ain't had reason to be scared by nothin' in a while." Vinnie understood; he was something Crash had only heard about in horror stories. "Betta to loyn you gotta panic problem before it happens inna fight though, eh?" Crash said it was almost nice, being afraid; he had felt more like a person since shedding his shell. Vinnie didn't think he got that anymore. "Things're a lot clearer now. Not so mucha da meat interferin'." Crash: "That clarity can be blinding. Any kinda power or authority comes at a cost."

**Outcomes**
- Item: Lady Soar's Water token (imprinted reishi fabric).
- Sylas: 5,000 from Commander Dahl.
