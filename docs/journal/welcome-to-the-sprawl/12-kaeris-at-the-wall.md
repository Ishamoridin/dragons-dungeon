# Session 12 — Kaeris at the Wall

## [S12.1] Burning Nimbus
- Campaign time: 3500-07-19T10:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Wall, west of the Spire-way

Vinnie launched himself like a bullet at Kaeris, meaning to grapple her and strike with Burning Nimbus while she was held. Her weaponry was perfect and she cut him for a hundred and twenty. He lit aflame where he was wounded, the edges of the cuts burning toward one another until they closed, went still as the flames died, and rose.

She was marked by Vexorion and nothing he threw could touch her: brawl, smoke, Reiatsu, five swings and five dodges, one of them while she was still talking. Then his resolve caught. He bit a chunk out of her. He came away with Heart in his mouth and the fury drained out of him all at once, replaced by the satisfaction of having hurt Flock's ally; he dispersed his excess smoke as a screen and followed the others.

**Outcomes**
- Vinnie: 120 damage taken, healed by self-immolation; one successful bite (305 damage) on Kaeris; 87 to Flock.

- DM: "Sorry, being shouted, brb one second, but map prepped."

## [S12.2] Wielding what cannot be
- Campaign time: 3500-07-19T11:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Wall

Vinnie tried to cast the stolen Heart as a binding, to pin Kaeris to the Wall. The casting failed, but it got rid of the Heart, and the Wall answered with Doom. The DM called it "Wielding what cannot be": resolve, presence and release to hold himself together, and then Doom damage that empties a number of elements of the character sheet, chosen by the player, that cannot be resisted or mitigated. Vinnie lost eight.

Vexorion himself turned to look at them. The intimidation alone was enough to bend knees. Vinnie's composure, somehow, was perfect.

Glitz, later: "So Vinnie, what was up with that heart thing?" "Bit a chunk out of Kaeris, ended up with some Heart, so I had to dump it." "...How...?" "Tried casting it to bind her to the wall. Didn't work, but it got rid of that shit."

**Outcomes**
- Vinnie: Doom damage, 8 sheet elements emptied (player's choice).
- Vexorion: intimidation observed at 33.

### Table notes
- Glitz's player acknowledged Glitz "did something probably not right" because a major flaw was triggered; Vinnie's player said the same of Vinnie and Kaeris.
- DM: "It's okay to come settlement (2 mins)."

## [S12.3] Cassian Andair and Flock's offer
- Campaign time: 3500-07-19T15:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Air settlement at the Wall

In the Air settlement Glitz asked a heavy whether General Cassian was too busy to speak. Sylas's presence carried the introductions. Flock, as Sovereign, laid an offer before Sylas and named it Evil's Rival: a permanent physiological adaptation, not a technique. Gain passive shapeshifting. Lose the ability to make positive social rolls. Gain two hundred development of his choice and ten thousand Evil. Minimum adaptation of three. Sylas did not answer yet.

**Outcomes**
- Offered to Sylas: "Evil's Rival" (shapeshifting; no positive social rolls; +200 development; +10,000 Evil).
