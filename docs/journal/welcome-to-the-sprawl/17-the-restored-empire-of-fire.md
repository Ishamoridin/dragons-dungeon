# Session 17 — The Restored Empire of Fire

## [S17.1] Blue flame from the eastern island
- Campaign time: 3500-09-29T10:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Imperial City of Fire

To the southeast a series of explosions and clashes broke across the partially restored city of the Restored Empire of Fire; gunfire and yelling flooded out of it. Streaks of blue flame rushed from the eastern island toward the Imperial City and the gunshots doubled. The Sire did not react to Sylas at all.

Fire gunners fired Entropic rounds. Gym dropped his golden aura and released his Partial, and his brawl climbed to forty-one. Doomed Air gunners ambushed from the flank for six hundred and eighty-four in one volley; Gym split the damage with reishi manipulation. A Fire archer's magma arrows dealt five hundred true. Glitz's ranged fire found its mark again and again, then he switched to control and curses. Sylas's counters reached thirty-seven.

Then Ma Gama. A Reiatsu modifier at forty, then forty-two enraged, then forty-four. He was Consumed by White Flame: every cost spread across all three bars, all damage trebled, all damage taken doubled, Rei and Fire techniques free, an extra major action for two hundred and fifty Will. Glitz's Reiatsu met it at thirty-two and the party ground him down.

**Outcomes**
- Ma Gama defeated. Technique observed: Consumed by White Flame.
- Gym: 2,493 and 922 Reiatsu-split damage absorbed; armour repaired repeatedly.
- Glitz: 25,750 essence in a single haul; chose Doom, Evil, Determination, Fire and Vitality.

### Table notes
- Gym's player ran the "armour repair" macro many times; the DM later ruled on its multiplier.

## [S17.2] "I'm the last rebel leader"
- Campaign time: 3500-09-29T16:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Imperial City of Fire

Aureon admitted quietly to Glitz: "...I'm the last rebel leader. I guess we need elections?" Glitz smiled. "If that's what you want! Completing a rebellion means you get to figure out the government reform." Aureon: "We never had a plan for one. Our entire goal was just to remove what was trying to claim Fire." Gym nodded sagely at Lazarus, having followed none of it.

Lazarus handed down realm boons for the city's deliverance: a hundred to a handful of development trees each, a hundred more to the maximum of Reiryoku and Will, a bonus to every Rei roll against Heart-Kin and their collaborators, and a tenth less damage from conditional sources.

Sylas nearly died. Six tests, several below the line; two dice against death and he beat it, then rolled for intensity and for the shape of the wound. What he received instead was a title: Sovereign of Magic, and the Sovereign version of Whisper of a Friend, "Leveraging Assets", which lets a Sovereign adopt the powers of any member of a Wave who agrees to full alliance or to defensive action.

Lazarus snatched a Surprise bird out of the air the instant it formed, went dazed for a moment, then smirked. "It seems 'The Rat' has several other Zodiac gathered at the Tavern." The Director sighed and recited: "Evil Tower #2 reports Sovereign Flock has been teleported to The Wandering Tavern one hour ago. Message includes a personal note of 'See you here.'"

**Outcomes**
- Realm boons: +100 to 1d4 development trees (Gym 2, Sylas 2, Glitz 3); +100 max Rei and Will; +1 Rei rolls vs Heart-Kin; -10% conditional damage.
- Sylas: Sovereign of Magic; technique "Leveraging Assets [Sovereign]".
- Glitz: tracked conditionals: took over 10% HP damage, resolve, faith. Gym: over 10% damage, zero damage, spirit weapon uses.

### Table notes
- Gym's player misread the boon count ("oh I only rolled 2"). Sylas: "BRB" and "Back" bookended the death rolls.
- Glitz asked what the rest rules were, since they weren't written down.
