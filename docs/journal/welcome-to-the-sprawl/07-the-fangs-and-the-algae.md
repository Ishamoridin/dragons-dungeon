# Session 07 — The Fangs and the algae

## [S07.1] Vinnie makes a made man
- Campaign time: 3500-07-08T09:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Fangs' camp, north of the township

A Fang scout slipped up to Vinnie and whispered: two squads crossing the bridge and some sort of delegate from the Nation of Evil, ten minutes out. Vinnie nodded. The Fang marksman yelled "You 'eard the boss!" and the whole gang sprinted into a defensive line along the north of the camp. "Find your targets and keep ready," Vinnie told them, "but nobody fires until I call it." Then he stared at Crash.

Vinnie reached into himself, pulled out ten thousand motes of Fire and handed them to the head Fang like a stick of gum. "Half to make yourself a Native and half to distribute among the gang. You're a made man now. I want you guys cultivating this at least an hour a day each, yeah?"

Crash argued that the Zodiac should flex too. A new boss was muscling in on everything to see where his territory ended, and territory is wherever nobody pushes back. Vinnie thought it was dumb to poke him without finishing the job, but he was in. Glitz sent a Surprise bird to Aureon warning that some of Vinnie's friends would visit and not to hit them too hard. Aureon's reply arrived five seconds later: "Anything is possible, my desert rose," with a crude sketch of a snake on fire.

Beside them a Vitalizard rolled its googly eyes up at Sylas and Glitz, waved its puncturing arms, crossed its eyes, belched tremendously and started squirming.

**Outcomes**
- Vinnie: 10,000 Fire given to the Fangs; the head Fang becomes a Native.
- The Fangs now answer to Vinnie.

### Table notes
- Glitz: "that's his response to 'hi?'" Several brb/back exchanges.

## [S07.2] Spores in the pool
- Campaign time: 3500-07-08T12:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The overgrown pool and the Water hub

The Truthseer offered a riddle, "What is given freely can be taken without intent," and a destination: Aether, town of Insight. Crash fell into the overgrowth doing tucked front flips the whole way down ("Oh sweet god, that's a lot of plants"). Myre arrived with a Rushguard captain and eight volunteers who had refused to leave him unprotected.

Glitz sent a Surprise bird to Commander Dahl: the algae in the pool might have spores on everyone who had been there, and it could spread into the river if troops were sent now. Dahl deployed the Relief Corps for quarantine, the Rushguard to clear the river and the Water Militia to civil defence. Glitz's luck held perfectly and he sent a second bird: send for help from Night; only a tier-two essence from destruction can beat this; do not try to use water, or Water falls. Dahl's release flared as he obeyed.

Vinnie braced to catch Glitz falling from the sky and looked up to find no Glitz falling on him.

**Outcomes**
- Water quarantines the pool and clears the river; Night is asked for help.
- Lead: Aether, town of Insight.

### Table notes
- Myre's player (the DM) stepped out for the dogs; players were told to declare actions in text.

## [S07.3] Lazarus and the rumours board
- Campaign time: 3500-07-08T17:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Water administrative hub

The crisis passed. Lazarus, the Angel, was standing among them, and when Crash explained a point of local politics he answered, "I'm sorry, that was like telling me how beige and grey differ. I see you had a point. It's alien to me." He slowly stopped smiling and just observed. Vinnie leaned on a wall, lit a cigarette and offered him one. Lazarus's hand waved it away before his mind did, and the gesture seemed to make him frown.

The Rushguard were tense: a marksman readied his rifle without aiming, skirmishers formed reishi spears. Glitz approached and waved. Crash pulled Vinnie aside: "Dude, Vinny. I don't know what to do. You saw what this guy made in there, and now look at him. Man, I'm worried." Vinnie: "Worry gets you nothing. Get prepared." Crash wanted a private word and then promised: he would protect Lazarus with his life, but he would tell the others after they were healed and after Lazarus left, so that if anyone came for him at camp they would beat their asses. "I just can't fuckin' lie to 'em, man. I don't believe in it."

The party was handed six packs of gourmet travel rations and the hub's rumours: a cave halfway to Earth that may be the original Rushguard training facility and may hold a Human-Killer weapon; three Air survivors sent by the Liar Bird, describing a wall of Gloom and Despair cutting the desert in two, and a Doom wall between Air and Water that Flock claims to have corrupted into Evil, so the way west is open but untamed; a Rushguard party slaughtered in the north by a masked individual wielding a First Resero, whose one survivor resigned and joined the Nation of Evil; and Water's proposal to the Night-Kin for a joint mass teleport into old Earth and Fire to open the Spireway from the far side.

Sylas mentioned he had traded away the steel they were given and now held a surplus of Water. Vinnie had an unexplained feeling he would soon be protecting Crash from a second hostile nation. Sylas: "'Crash the Terrorist'. Title achieved."

**Outcomes**
- Item: 6x gourmet travel rations (4 weeks for 6 people, 4 burden each, 24 total).
- Party: 20,045 essence credited.
- Rumours recorded as above.
