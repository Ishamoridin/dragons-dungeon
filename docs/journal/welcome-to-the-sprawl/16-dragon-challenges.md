# Session 16 — Dragon challenges

## [S16.1] Lorish, Merak, Landier, Mosh
- Campaign time: 3500-09-22T10:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The volcano city, Fire

The Dragons of Fire tested the Zodiac the only way Dragons do. Lorish led with presence and a dodge nobody could beat. Merak threw spears and dealt Dragon damage in the hundreds, four strikes at a time. Landier's Reiryoku control was a wall at thirty-plus, and his impacts came in threes. Mosh knocked Gym back twice and accelerated with every blow.

Gym fought the way he lives: straight in, brawl at thirty, thirty-two, taking knockbacks and coming back grinning. Glitz's Reiatsu modifier hit thirty-five and his beams landed for three hundred and sixteen; his constitution kept him upright under Dragon fire, and he never once tried to leave. Sylas's counters at thirty-eight, twice in a row, turned Merak's own force back on him and his heavy strikes passed five hundred. Mosh's piety failed where his fists had not.

Landier, satisfied, taught Stalk Prey: no more targeting limits for Dragon or Magma techniques, and they can be used as a minor action, at half again the cost.

**Outcomes**
- Technique: Stalk Prey (Dragon) (Dragon/Magma techniques untargeted, usable as minor, +50% cost).
- Gym: knocked back repeatedly; stayed in the fight.

### Table notes
- Gym: "I'm not your mate." Glitz's player noted Glitz never tried to run; "he's just enjoying the battle."

## [S16.2] Lantern, Lazarus and the tempering
- Campaign time: 3500-09-22T15:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The volcano city

Lantern, a Dragon with infamy weighing on his resolve, graded them on an average of constitution, control, adaptation, zeal, piety, occult and wisdom. Sylas's zeal was high; Gym's wisdom was a liability and his politics worse. Lazarus graded again, harder, and added luck, three times over for Surprise. Gym's survival and adaptation carried him; his wisdom dragged everything else down.

The results were temperings: by Drive, by Drive and Doom in equal measure, by Fixation. Yu Byrne glowered through all of it. Verbem, present again, met the Dragons' tests with resolve and zeal and a final roll of pure luck that could not have gone better. Thorne, the traitor now, worked the room with expression and manipulation.

Gym's left arm did not come through whole. From now on every physical roll that used an arm carried a penalty.

**Outcomes**
- Temperings offered: Drive; Drive & Doom (50/50); Fixation.
- Gym: arm injury. All physical rolls involving an arm at -1.
- Glitz: 2,000 Light, 5,000 Nature, 1,000 Beast, 1,000 Determination. Sylas: 50,000 Nature.

### Table notes
- DM: "Sorry, being shouted, brb." Sylas took a bio break. Gym's player replied "Np" with a stray brace.
