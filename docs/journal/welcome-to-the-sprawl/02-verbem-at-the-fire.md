# Session 02 — Verbem at the fire

## [S02.1] The man made of shadow
- Campaign time: 3500-07-01T22:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Camp at the top of the stairs

A civilian had been watching the four of them for a while when a man six feet tall, broad but elegant, wrapped in something halfway between black leather and living shadow, walked up behind the onlooker and nodded once. The civilian ran. The man turned to study the group on the stairs, said nothing, and later introduced himself as Verbem of the Night-Kin.

He did not threaten. He stood beside a tent and cooed at the creature inside it, and maroon motes gathered in his open palm as he harvested Beast from it with the ease of long practice. When the Zodiac began to compare notes on what they thought the world was, he raised a hand and offered, gently: "Very accurate, but not complete. I can share what the Night-Kin know, if you wish."

### Table notes
- Sylas and Glitz greeted Verbem's entrance with "WHAT IS GOING ON!!!" and a "blue da ba dee" joke. Sylas raised a hand for a rules question.
- The DM stepped away briefly: barking dogs, then a cold drink before the scene proper.

## [S02.2] Cultivation lessons and a smoke that flexes
- Campaign time: 3500-07-01T23:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Camp fire

Vinnie sat by the fire smoking and tried to cultivate what little Night he had. The essence slipped away from him twice, once so badly that it lashed back at him and Verbem stared as if to ask how such a thing could happen. Vinnie answered with the cockiest grin he could manage. Verbem, unmoved, demonstrated what a cultivation hex looks like in the hands of a Night-Kin and the difference was humiliating.

Glitz fared better, drawing on the Nature zone around the camp and harvesting a clean handful of it. Verbem tested each of them with his aura and got nothing back from Glitz but static. "Too open-minded for a Night-Kin," he said. "You might as well speak to me in Aramaic."

Vinnie, bored, shaped his smoke into words and then into a tiny muscular man who flexed at Glitz until Glitz, baffled, flexed back and gave a thumbs up. Crash held up a finger and showed everyone the notes on his interface: twice a session he could grant every ally a bonus for a turn, and once a turn he could hand free actions to a handful of them. "This is what I can do, by the way, friendos."

Verbem shared the measure of a Night-Kin body of his kind: average physique, sharper mind, and pools of four hundred health and five hundred each of Reiryoku and Will. The Zodiac finally understood how far below the locals they started.

**Outcomes**
- Glitz: Nature harvested from the camp zone.
- Vinnie: two failed cultivations, one with backlash; Reiatsu tested at 18+ by Verbem.
- Party learns Verbem's baseline (physical 5 / social 7 / mental 10; HP 400, Reiryoku 500, Will 500).

### Table notes
- DM away twice for stressed dogs and a family argument. Vinnie's player posted a Big Bird trivia fact while waiting.
- Confusion over whether the smoke flex was in or out of character; resolved as "both".
- Glitz's player noted he had been heavy on Reiatsu as a previous character, Iggy.

## [S02.3] Breaking camp
- Campaign time: 3500-07-02T08:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Camp

Morning. Crash packed the camp while Vinnie lit another cigarette and watched. "Ey Vin, you haven't answered yet. You wanna go fuck up some bandits, or some scavs, or head straight to the plant place?" Vinnie decided fucking up some dirtbags sounded like just the ticket, and told him to lead the way. Crash suggested asking the locals, and the four argued about who should do the talking. Glitz won the vote on the grounds that he knew the place.

### Table notes
- Votes were cast in chat, then settled by a round of coin-flip rolls.
- Vinnie's player: "You're not meant to put jam on the body of Christ, Matty."
