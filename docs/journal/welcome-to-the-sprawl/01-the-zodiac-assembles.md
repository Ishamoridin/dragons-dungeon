# Session 01 — The Zodiac assembles

## [S01.1] Essences drawn
- Campaign time: 3500-07-01T19:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Southern Sprawl, the stairs above the wilds

The Wall had chosen four strangers to be the newest Zodiac, and the first thing it asked of them was to discover what they were made of. Out beyond the last lit street, where nobody stops a stabbing and the only law is the knife, the four reached inward for the essence that had been waiting there all along.

Vinnie found Night, thin and quiet, barely thirteen motes of it. Crash Vandal found Night too, a fat sixty-nine motes that pleased him far more than it should have. The man who would be called Sylas found Gloom, a hundred and seventy-three motes thick, and Glitz found Beast, which the Wall rewarded with a fifth again for its wildness.

**Outcomes**
- Vinnie: 13 Night (tier 2). Crash: 69 Night (tier 2). Sylas: 173 Gloom (tier 1). Glitz: 70 Beast (tier 1, with the Beast bonus).

## [S01.2] What they carried in
- Campaign time: 3500-07-01T20:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Southern Sprawl

Each paid for their past out of the essence they had just found. Vinnie kept it simple: a leather jacket and a knife-pistol he called the Reishooter. Glitz matched the jacket and added military boots. Sylas spent Gloom lavishly on a full suit of plated European armour, chest to gloves. Crash, who had clearly been a detective before he had been anything else, bought a laser revolver he insisted on calling a proper space gun, a Megacorp set of helmet, plate, greaves, boots and gloves, a backpack, a pouch, a Target-brand charm and his old badge, and was left with four motes of Night to his name.

Then the Wall gave each of them a spirit weapon. Crash's manifested as an infinite deck of eight kinds of card: ally strikes, borrowed armour, free movement, healing, shared spells, a position swap, free minor actions and a coin-flip that refunds or grants a major action. Glitz's was stranger: three rock-star poses. A headbang to heighten the battle, crossed arms to calm it, and a guitar solo to drag every eye in the room onto him.

**Outcomes**
- Starting gear generated on each sheet as listed above.
- Crash Vandal: card-deck spirit weapon (4d8 hand on summon, one card per major action).
- Glitz: pose spirit weapon (headbang, arms crossed, guitar solo).
