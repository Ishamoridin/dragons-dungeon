# Session 21 — Max pressure

## [S21.1] Dragon harvesting
- Campaign time: 3500-10-13T10:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz
- Location: The Wandering Tavern training grounds

Gym was told his training was sufficient and took it badly. Then he asked to harvest Dragon at maximum pressure. The Wall required resolve, zeal, empathy, control and adaptation and warned that anything above six on the final die was exceedingly dangerous. Gym rolled a three, held his resolve at twenty-nine, and his adaptation and constitution carried the rest. He repaired afterwards with the times-eight multiplier and the numbers ran into the thousands: twenty-three hundred to health, eleven hundred to Reiryoku, six hundred to Will. Sylas gained a single point of Will and read the occult of it perfectly.

**Outcomes**
- Gym: Dragon harvested at max pressure without disaster. Repairs: 2,336 HP, 1,152 Rei, 632 Will, then 944 / 1,048 / 832.
- Sylas: Willpower +1.

## [S21.2] Trading with the Rooster
- Campaign time: 3500-10-13T14:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz
- Location: The Wandering Tavern

Lazarus tested Gym on politics, presence and analysis, which went as well as politics ever does for Gym. Then Gym found Pluck the Rooster of Order and, lacking a shared language, wrote in fire. "WHAT LIMITS YOU WANT?" "OH, NOT ENOUGH ENTROPY." He showed his essence screen. "GOT ANY ENTROPY TO TRADE? I CAN TRADE 400 CREATION."

Pluck's first attempt failed, his exo-suit brawl landing at thirty-five and thirty-seven, and Gym's counter echoed for fourteen hundred, then more. Pluck adjusted. Gym wrote, "Can I get some of everything? I'm curious and can trade, you know what I got," pulled out a single spark worth twenty-five hundred Entropy, and pulled it apart. Pluck listed the Uncanny pairings he knew: Insight and Night, Courage and Beast, Gloom and Resolve, Camouflage and Beast, Ambition and Vitality, Corruption and Night. Then four words: Rune, Law, Absolution, Permanence. Gym answered: "Rune of Power, Law of Progress, Absolution of Weakness, Permanence of Progress." Pluck's engine ran five releases in a row, and Gym and Glitz adapted to every one.

**Outcomes**
- Gym: 2,500 Entropy spent as a spark; Uncanny recipes learned; "Rune of Power, Law of Progress, Absolution of Weakness, Permanence of Progress" declared.
- Pluck's tests: control, manipulation, release, resolve, adaptation, constitution, luck, kharma.
