# Session 15 — Gym

## [S15.1] "It's all white"
- Campaign time: 3500-09-15T10:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Director's chamber, Shimmering Sands

The Wall gave the survivors a single mote of Harmony: "By you yesterday, for use today, to shape tomorrow. A gift from the Eighth, Ninth and Director, Harmony to deny the Heart." Glitz posted the tour list on the wall: deal with the Gloom in the palace; deal with Shady Sands and collect the Human-Killer weapon; find the Insight-Kin realm; find the Wandering Tavern; find the Tea House.

The new Zodiac, Gym, Weasel of Ambition, was barely listening. He rotated slowly in place staring at the opulence like a bumpkin in a big city and mumbled "Nice place..." Crash walked up and shook his hand firmly. "Hey there, bud. How much do you know about the world and what's going on?" Gym snapped out of it: "Oh hi! I know some stuff! Gramps explained all about what you guys have been up to. He tells me I'm one of you now?" Crash: "Seems to be! Heart-Kin causin' problems for the world and we're causing problems for them." Gym: "Yeah, let's deck 'em in the schnozz!" Crash warned they were talky wise-guys who would twist how he thinks. "Aaaa, that's no good. I hate talking too much. Fightin's much simpler and a bunch more fun."

The Director raised an eyebrow, smirked, and imitated a nosebleed.

**Outcomes**
- Party: 1 Harmony.
- Gym joins the Zodiac.

### Table notes
- Gym's first words in chat: "It's all white."

## [S15.2] Block this
- Campaign time: 3500-09-15T11:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Director's chamber

Crash turned a chair around and sat facing Gym. "Show me your spirit weapon." "Oh, that's tricky. It's me. We can go fight if you wanna see it?" Crash explained the categories, reishi armour, spirit body, curses, summons, and Gym tried: "If someone tries to hurt me I hit them back, maybe even stop 'em, and the more I do it the better I get. If I do really well I can knock 'em out, kapow!"

Crash: "Come at me, man." Gym picked himself up off the wall, unhurt, said "Hey, no sucker punches!", stamped his feet so the air billowed as if in an updraft and launched himself across the room. Crash blocked, called "Byakurai!", and fired. Gym's Reiatsu held. Crash blew on his fingers. "Yeah, you seem ready to fight already. We're ready to go out and beat ass." The Director droned: "Steps of ceremony and performance that add up to a theatrical combined execution of intention, incomplete and deeply flawed explanation. Ritual is a Human concept."

Sylas asked whether he might sit with Vinnie's mote of power and reach into it with his Reiryoku, and rolled the worst possible result.

**Outcomes**
- Gym: spirit weapon confirmed as self (counter-brawling, escalating).

### Table notes
- Gym's player: "Yes, I'm very aware that Gym just described fighting." Crash: "This happens every time you poke the bear, lord."

## [S15.3] Fire is a warzone
- Campaign time: 3500-09-15T14:00
- Present: Gym, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Through the Tower to Fire

Mosh struck the Tower from the far side and the whole structure shuddered like a minor earthquake. The field over the doorway gave way to molten magma, to stone, to ash, to air. Beyond it lay an entire shattered eastern city built into an active volcano, and dozens of figures trading arrows, bullets, swords and fists. Fire was a warzone. Crash whispered "Yesss..." and Gym, startled by the tone shift, obeyed, saluted, started moving south and whispered to Mosh, "I don't know, I'm new."

Lu-Flame was the one to fear. His panic-cast Final Blow hit for three hundred and fourteen, his magma beams for nearly four hundred each, and his enraged release climbed into the forties. Fire warriors, gunners, an archer and a rebel weaver pressed from every side. Gym's brawl found its rhythm late and then landed two thirty-sevens in a row. Glitz's Reiatsu answered beam for beam. Sylas's weapon counters reached thirty-eight. Crash tallied damage past three hundred a strike with his will behind it.

In the middle of it the DM noted a new quality in Crash: Inviolable Fuel. He was now immune to consumptive actions, magma, lit and corruptive instant kills included, and would take at most a hundred to each bar from any of them.

Afterwards Glitz asked the weekly question, how much do we make between weeks, and Gym recited the font rate. The Director told Gym he would die halfway up the Tower, or at three hundred feet from Harmony exposure. "So you're saying there's a chance." "...No." "If I fell off, would you catch me?" "Yes." "Then I can just let go when it gets too much!" "When it gets too much you'll die. There won't be time to let go." Gym nodded seriously, bounced on his toes, and muttered to himself, "Cruel?" Aureon handed over three Re-sparks.

**Outcomes**
- Crash: "Inviolable Fuel" (consumptive and critical instant kills capped at 100 to each bar).
- Item: 3x Re-spark (1 burden each).
- Between-weeks income rule: 1,008 x font rate per 10.
