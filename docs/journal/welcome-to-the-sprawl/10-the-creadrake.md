# Session 10 — The CreaDrake

## [S10.1] Guardian against Vexorion
- Campaign time: 3500-07-11T16:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Wall, northeast of the township

The CreaDrake was two thousand health of Creation and Magic, armoured to a hundred and twenty-five, and the Wall itself understood it as "Guardian against Vexorion, denier of the artery". It opened with two strength-driven blows, then Creation Breath for a hundred and forty-five. The Spire Warden fought beside it, mirroring any technique he had seen once that day.

Vinnie's smoke, stacked ten deep, hit for a hundred and thirty-three and then kept hitting. He landed two natural twenties back to back and his doubled bursts of two hundred and seventy-three and two hundred and six tore through the drake's defence. Sylas countered its ground-shaking ripple ("YOU LEAVE THE GROUND ALONE, YOU BEAST") and converted the difference into a three-hundred-and-eighty-four strike. Glitz's curses stripped its armour and could not be resisted until its own turn. Crash drew eights.

The drake breathed four Lesser Creation Breaths at once. Vinnie was engulfed in prismatic energy and it washed off him, leaving him unscathed and grinning cockily. Then the drake wove Creation into a bite and swallowed him. For a round Vinnie was a lump in its throat, making faint noises of struggle on the way down.

**Outcomes**
- CreaDrake: HP 800/2000 mid-fight, weakest defence Defend, weakest to Doom, Despair and Entropy.
- Vinnie: survived four Creation Breaths unharmed; swallowed whole.

### Table notes
- Debate on whether "defend" is useless given visible damage; consensus that you choose after seeing the roll.
- Glitz on the swallow: "did you actually restrict his FOV? That's cool as fuck." Glitz noted the music is diegetic.

## [S10.2] Thornmantle's weight and the kill
- Campaign time: 3500-07-11T17:30
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Wall

A second Thornmantle joined and pressed Nature's weight down on the party for a hundred and sixty-seven, then three hundred and seventy-eight. Glitz answered the pressure with expression rather than faith and held. Crash's deck turned the tide twice, granting the free actions that let Sylas land his five-strike at a thirty, and Vinnie, free of the throat, poured three smoke bursts in a row at a hundred and forty-plus each. The CreaDrake took a Creation short rest and it was not enough.

When it died, the Wall paid out in Creation's currency. Each of them rolled against fifty thousand and came away with tens of thousands; then thousands more; then hundreds; then a last handful. Sylas took Water. The DM named the best of it: the Chit of the Current, a small carved piece of wood invested with a hundred thousand Water over a very long time. Whoever carries it speaks for Water and is aided by Water, so long as they never act against it. Every Water leader holds one: Soar, Dahl, Yu, Marr.

Four Reiryoku books came out of the rubble, and a Kido/Hado manual, number 101, by Gerye. Vinnie called it a night. Crash collected two beers on the way out.

**Outcomes**
- Essence: Sylas 49,276 / 494 / 196 / 13; Glitz 43,969 / 3,948 / 158 / 45; Vinnie 30,580 / 2,997 / 84 / 22; Crash 39,683 / 2,976 / 254 / 38 (tiers descending).
- Sylas: 100 Miracle, 100 Magic, 100 Inevitable, 5 Creation.
- Item: Chit of the Current (burden 1).
- Books: Reiryoku Reactive Defense, Reiryoku Density Defenses, Acting Through Invisible You, Self-Shaped Stance (1 burden each); "Kido/Hado #101" by Gerye.
