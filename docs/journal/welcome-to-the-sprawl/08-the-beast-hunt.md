# Session 08 — The beast hunt

## [S08.1] Rotted tracks and a ripple from the east
- Campaign time: 3500-07-10T09:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Rock fields north of the township

Crash drank two hundred motes of Water and went to work the way a detective does: between the buildings, in the squats, along the rock. The tracks he found were not human. Drops of rotted flesh and claw marks ran from the southwest toward the northeast, and they stayed bestial the whole way; he had half expected the Rat to be something else. The Gloom clinging to them was wild and natural, not anyone's in particular. The scramble led into a tunnel through the wall.

Vinnie passed round the dossier he had been given. Known Heart-Kin: Kaeris of the Fallen Pack, exiled Beast-Kin, information broker and manipulator, retreated to Earth. Suspected: Raeva of the Rushing Avalanche, Earth-Kin swordswoman who vanished as Precipice fell, now in the Township of Water; and Vexorion of the Ruins, the masked figure who personally broke the old armies of Fire, Air and Earth, wandering the northern and western reaches. The nature of Heart: an unstable alternative to Harmony, needing a quarter-million of a tier one, or five hundred Light, to touch without corruption.

Then the air rippled. For a moment it blurred into motion and a wave of spent potential rolled out from nearly due east, leaving a sense of permanent calm where there had been primal excitement. Raeva, when asked, chose to wait while they hunted.

**Outcomes**
- Dossier: Known Heart-Kin (Kaeris; suspected Raeva and Vexorion) recorded.
- Event: ripple of spent potential from the east.

### Table notes

- Votes on continue versus hunt: hunt won. Vinnie's player had a sneezing fit.

## [S08.2] Galehopper, Marchwing, Thornmantle, Grudgeshell
- Campaign time: 3500-07-10T11:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The tunnel and the hollow beyond

Four beasts waited. A Galehopper that hit like a hammer and spat flat force. A Marchwing whose strikes ignored armour entirely; Sylas took three of them and kept dancing. A Thornmantle that drained essence by the hundreds and crushed with Nature's weight. A Grudgeshell that barely landed a blow.

Vinnie's smoke dragon took shape above him, and his smoke struck in threes for the first time, then harder. Crash summoned the deck and dealt free actions and heals. Glitz headbanged without pause and the whole fight ran faster for it; he nearly lost himself in the song twice. Sylas was the engine of it: with a four-strike combination and the dance's multiplier he dealt three hundred and sixty-nine, then more, and his final burst of two hundred and fifty vaporised the Thornmantle outright. The Vitalizard pouted and dug at the floor when it went.

The DM's "strategic combination" of beasts hit the party for seven hundred and eighty-four in one exchange. They all stood up afterwards.

**Outcomes**
- Thornmantle destroyed; the rest driven off or killed.
- Grudgeshell yields Beast, Order, Insight and Nature.
- Vinnie: took 471 essence drain from the Thornmantle.

### Table notes

- Glitz asked whether keeping the dance going counts as spirit-weapon use each turn. Roll20 choked on nested multipliers; three players re-rolled the same formula.

## [S08.3] Spiadanse and a windfall of impossible essence
- Campaign time: 3500-07-10T14:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The hollow

Spiadanse was standing, bolt upright, plating gouged in several places and missing his left hand from the wrist down. His right hand rested, trembling, on the hilt of his bastard sword. His shield hung across his back because he could no longer hold it. "So what happened here?" Glitz asked. Sylas smiled at Crash: "Come on, the grass feels great."

Glitz's Reiatsu peaked at exactly the wrong and right moment. The cascade of chaos rolled through the beast's remains and crystallised into things that should not exist yet: sixteen thousand each of Uncanny, Infallibility and Triumph, and eight thousand of Light.

Vinnie declared his first repair: starting with the skin, using Fire, fifteen hundred to do it in one action. The DM allowed it as a known technique. He sighed in relief as his outer surface returned to normal.

**Outcomes**
- Glitz: 16,222 Uncanny, 16,222 Infallibility, 16,222 Triumph (unstable tier 2), 8,111 Light.
- Vinnie: skin repaired with 1,500 Fire (rule: 100 Reiryoku first turn, +50 per extra turn).
- Spiadanse: left hand lost.

### Table notes
- Sylas: "I'm bad at fighting them when I am dancing."
