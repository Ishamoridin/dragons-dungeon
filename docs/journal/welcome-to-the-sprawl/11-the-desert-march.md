# Session 11 — The desert march

## [S11.1] Marr, a note from Z, and a quarrel
- Campaign time: 3500-07-18T10:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Township of Water

A week passed in the township. Merchant Marr, who could not pronounce "triumvirate" ("Try-Uhm-Ver-at"), spoke for Water's trade. Someone had scrawled "super bloc" on a wall, and under it a note: "At Air's Despair, a refuge is found, we'll see you there. - Z."

Myre and Flock were both present when the talk turned to the tower idea, and Glitz, who had been ambivalent, chose to speak for Water. Flock looked slowly at Sylas and mouthed, without a sound, "YOU'VE decided..." Crash sat down, lit a smoke and said, "Alright, let's talk about it then, bud. What problems you have with me?" Sylas: "As Glitz said, you go off on your own a lot and do your own thing a lot." Crash protested that outside the Heart-Kin conversation he had never left them once on a quest. Sylas: "You ventured around the area just before the CreaDrake instead of talking to Verbem."

## [S11.2] The Fractured Mountains
- Campaign time: 3500-07-18T14:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The desert beyond the northern Water ruins

The party crossed the desert in a constant, grim march. Rushguard lined the road, then gave way to the resplendent and far more imposing guard of the Spire-kin. After almost another hour the skeletal reishi shacks turned to verdant grass and the skeletons of marble structures, and above them rose the Fractured Mountains: six peaks of one former mountain, shattered so that each leaks a deeply unstable tertiary essence into the world. At their foot stood abandoned buildings, the far outline of an intact marble gateway, and a spherical marble reishi facility over which Lazarus the Angel levitated, four wings outstretched, eyes closed.

Spialen moved ahead into protective range of the sphere. Lazarus opened his eyes and straightened. Spialen saluted, half-bowed and stepped back for Rune, who turned his eyes on the Zodiac and quietly watched. Somewhere beyond them, Vexorion's control of the Reiryoku around the mountain was flawless.

Crash came back to Glitz after his turn: "Weird. They all say the Heart-Kin are enemies of harmony, specifically HUMAN harmony, and they may be native constructs. By these folk." Glitz: "It's just what the humans did but from the other side, right? The towers were a nanomachine grey goo spreading and stifling, this is an organic version. A living virus."

**Outcomes**
- Rule: +1 to between-session rolls while in the Spire-way.

## [S11.3] Training in the Spire-way
- Campaign time: 3500-07-18T19:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Spire-way facility

Glitz came out of Resero when the training ended. Vinnie stayed in Partial Resero, his Smoke Dragon more detailed than before, hovering above and behind him. Investigating the structure, Vinnie could not work out who had learned to build this way. It was not pure reishi and barely felt man-made, as dense as the sand around it yet far more durable, shaped by a ripple effect rather than built. The Wall had formed it in response to something. Reishi-wise the whole structure was not worth one ingot.

The place tested them: resolve, occult, constitution. Anyone whose constitution fell short took a hundred true damage to health and will. Glitz's occult understanding was perfect; Vinnie's body was iron, twice over. Sylas just scraped by.

**Outcomes**
- Vinnie: Partial Resero sustained; Smoke Dragon gains detail.
- Finding: the Spire-way sphere was formed by the Wall, not constructed.
