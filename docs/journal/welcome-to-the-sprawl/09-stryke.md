# Session 09 — Stryke

## [S09.1] The Liar Bird, wounded
- Campaign time: 3500-07-11T10:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Township of Water

Stryke came for Flock. His release was heavier than anything the party had felt from a living thing and his intimidation hit the whole street. Flock answered with zeal and took a wound for it, then released harder through the pain. The Zodiac held their nerve, mostly: Crash froze, Glitz held his composure, Vinnie stood like a wall.

Afterwards Sylas found that threads of reishi and Steel had grown between his spirit and the reishi of his eyes. Even with their own essence gone they would see perfectly. He would not be blinded again.

**Outcomes**
- Sylas: Reishi Network (Eyes). Immune to most blinding effects and injuries.
- Flock: wounded (resolve lowered, release raised while wounded).

## [S09.2] "Now!"
- Campaign time: 3500-07-11T15:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Township of Water

Somewhere to the northeast there was a faint yell of "Now!" Then the world shook, violently. A yell, and silence. A Spire Warden stepped out of the dust, reishi gathering around his hands, and the party fell into initiative.
