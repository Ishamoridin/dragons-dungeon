# Session 13 — The Doom barrier

## [S13.1] The King of Air and the price of a wall
- Campaign time: 3500-07-20T10:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Air settlement; the Doom barrier

Cassian looked southeast, raised his eyebrows, then looked down at the Air motes orbiting his hand. They were cultivating themselves, slowly, and he was clearly not doing it. King Hasss spoke for Air with the polish of long politics and a persuasion nobody could argue with.

Glitz reached for the barrier first and nearly died for it: his release was strong but his body gave out and he collapsed to one knee. The Wall set the cost of dropping the Doom barrier: six thousand health, six thousand Reiryoku and six thousand Will, paid between them, with universal points tradeable at two hundred and fifty each if a tab could not be met. Glitz, barely alive, could give a hundred health, two hundred Reiryoku, two hundred Will. Vinnie put up thirteen hundred, nine hundred and two hundred and fifty. Sylas three hundred and fifty-nine, five hundred and fifty-nine, two hundred and forty-four. Crash emptied his Will. They paid it.

Thirty seconds after the barrier fell the whispers of sand gave way to distant gunfire, explosions and running feet. Cassian swept past with six Air Guard down the route the Zodiac had cleared: "We'll meet you in the city when you're ready. Trust us, compadres, you've done the hard part for us!"

**Outcomes**
- Doom barrier down. Party paid roughly 1,828 Reiryoku and the rest in HP, Will and universal points (6,250 removed).
- Glitz: reduced to 0 HP, survived.

### Table notes
- Glitz: "Literally death otherwise." Both Glitz and Crash paid partly from skills to avoid redoing their bars.
- Sylas: "Do player name, become an NPC." Glitz was surprised to find 142 Reiatsu manipulation development on his sheet.

## [S13.2] Doomed liberators
- Campaign time: 3500-07-20T12:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: The Air city approaches

Stryke's puppets held the approach: Doomed Liberators, Doomed Air heavies and gunners firing Entropy rounds that ignored every defence. Vinnie met the heavy's brawl with his own and won by a point. Sylas's counters climbed into the forties. Glitz danced on Doom with expression and luck, then paid for it. A Liberator III fired two shots that would each have killed anyone they landed on cleanly.

Vinnie gathered his smoke around himself in tightening currents until it coalesced into solid bands of reishi around his chest and arms: temporary ingots. Crash took a breath and pushed the concept of dodging into his aura, calling it "follow my lead", and it took. Vinnie proved he could do the same, then let Crash shoulder it until he needed to take over. Sylas healed thirty-eight to everyone's Reiryoku and Will, twice.

Glitz sent word to the Air kid they'd met: "We're gonna have to kill Stryke for a better Doom Zodiac, his words not mine. He admitted defeat but we still have to kill him. He's weak to his own Doom. We teleported his Doom thing to one of his puppets and it hurt him bad."

**Outcomes**
- Vinnie: temporary reishi ingots from smoke; 272 and 237 damage bursts.
- Crash: "Follow my lead: dodge" aura technique activated.
- Sylas: party healed +38 Reiryoku and Will, twice.

### Table notes
- Glitz: "Wait, the fucking fog of war can be a custom image? That's cool as fuck."
- Glitz almost forgot to pay a technique cost.

## [S13.3] Spoils, a new Sovereign, and a tertiary essence
- Campaign time: 3500-07-21T10:00
- Present: Vinnie, Sylas, Glitz, Crash Vandal
- Location: Air palace

King Hasss tested each of them in turn and tapped at the displays around him afterwards, humming, taking stock of what he was now responsible for. Sylas's faith and Reiryoku control impressed him; Crash's luck was abysmal and his politics perfect.

The party divided the spoils. Vinnie put three hundred and eighty into spirit weapon uses. Sylas split his between the weapon and Reiryoku. Glitz spent on occult, cultivation and presence and banked the reward for having been reduced to zero. Vinnie flatly refused to acknowledge any mention of his "previous incarnation", and said the new Doom Outsider, Carrion, should be killed immediately. Boros issued Sylas the technique "Not a Human" and Glitz "Agnostic Reishi". Vinnie received a five-piece suit with a Human-conditional set bonus: the Ferno Killer Suit, also called The Butcher's Suit.

Then Vinnie wove an unstable secondary into a newly stabilised tier one and made something tertiary: fire magnified by fire, magnified by everything fire could be. Carrion named it Dragon and called it ludicrously unstable. Each point cost five hundred thousand tier one, or two hundred tier two, or forty thousand tier three. Vinnie poured in five hundred Miracle, four thousand Resolve, four thousand Vitality and two thousand Light and came away with forty-four points of Dragon. Sylas, elsewhere, asked Asylum quietly what the difference was between the Goat of the Zodiac and the Outsider of Nature, because he knew the Goat was dying.

**Outcomes**
- Sylas: technique "Not a Human". Glitz: technique "Agnostic Reishi".
- Vinnie: Ferno Killer Suit / The Butcher's Suit (5/5); 44 Dragon essence (tertiary).
- Rule: Dragon costs 500,000 tier 1, 200 tier 2 or 40,000 tier 3 per point.
